# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Focus on safety, privacy, and educational value.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Sharing real biometric or sensitive data.
- Attempting to bypass safety constraints.

## Enforcement
Report issues to the maintainers. Repeated violations may result in participation restrictions.
